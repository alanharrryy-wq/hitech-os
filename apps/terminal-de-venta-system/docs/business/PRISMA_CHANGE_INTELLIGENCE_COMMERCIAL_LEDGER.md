# 3. ROI Engine ✅

Governing principle:

> **PRISMA Change Intelligence must be able to justify its price using engineering time alone. Risk reduction is upside, not the sales gimmick.**

Hard ROI:

1. Discovery recovered = engineers × discovery hours saved × loaded hourly cost.
2. Rework avoided = avoided events × average rework hours × loaded hourly cost.
3. Agent supervision reduced = agent tasks × review minutes saved / 60 × reviewer hourly cost.
4. Release/evidence efficiency = releases × hours saved/release × loaded hourly cost.

Optional risk layer only from customer-supplied historical data.

Equations:

- **Measured Monthly Benefit** = Discovery + Rework + Agent Supervision + Release/Evidence
- **Net Monthly Value** = Benefit − Price
- **ROI %** = `(Benefit − Price) / Price × 100`

Preferred selling metrics:

- Break-Even Time
- Engineering Capacity Recovered

Before/after pilot metrics:

- Mean Context Discovery Time
- Mean Change Scope Identification Time
- Mean Human Agent Supervision
- Out-of-Scope Change Rate
- Reopened Work Rate
- Evidence Assembly Time

# 6. Pricing & Subscription ✅ — V1.1 Founding Customer Amendment

## Governing pricing principle

> **Do not confuse value with entry friction. Preserve the standard-price hypothesis while lowering the first-customer commitment required to prove the value.**

The first validation cohort optimizes for external proof of repeatable value, not maximum first-contract ARPU.

## Founding validation cohort

Initial target: **first 3 external customers/design partners**.

### SKU 1 — Founding Repository Intelligence Pilot
**MXN $9,900 + IVA**, one-time.

- one repository or one clearly bounded monorepo scope
- Discover-led engagement with bounded Guard/Control demonstrations
- fixed scope and fixed price
- structured ROI/accuracy measurement
- founding/design-partner terms must receive useful consideration where practical: measurement rights, structured feedback, case-study/reference rights or deliberately reduced scope

**Mature/reference Pilot target:** **MXN $35,000 + IVA**, one-time, after external willingness-to-pay and ROI evidence supports it.

Do not offer a full pilot for free. A free or symbolic pre-evaluation may be used only as a narrow qualification/demo step.

### SKU 2 — Founding Guard validation period
For a customer that completes a founding pilot and demonstrates recurring Guard need:

**MXN $6,900/month + IVA for the first 3 months.**

Initial bounded scope remains:

- one primary repo/system boundary
- baseline refresh
- up to 4 bounded Change Readiness Packs/month
- drift/unknown/evidence changes
- one monthly technical review
- ROI continuation

Purpose:

- reduce initial adoption friction
- measure recurring use
- prove monthly ROI
- observe operator/support burden
- obtain real renewal evidence

This introductory rate is temporary and must not silently become permanent list pricing.

### Standard Guard target after validation
**MXN $12,500/month + IVA**

Transition to the standard target should be justified by measured recurring value, not merely elapsed time. If ROI is not demonstrated, investigate product value, packaging, buyer fit and use frequency before assuming the answer is simply another discount.

The prior **MXN $125,000/year + IVA prepaid** founding annual hypothesis is not the first-cohort offer under V1.1. Re-evaluate annual prepay only after recurring value and renewal behavior are observed.

### SKU 3 — Control Design Partner
Not public self-serve yet.

**Target design-partner range: MXN $25,000–$40,000/month + IVA** after its own readiness evidence.

No change to Control pricing in V1.1.

## Pricing evidence ladder

1. **Hypothesis** — internal willingness-to-pay theory only.
2. **Founding paid** — external customer pays the MXN $9,900 founding Pilot.
3. **Recurring conversion** — Pilot converts to Guard at MXN $6,900/month.
4. **Renewal evidence** — Guard continues after the 3-month validation period.
5. **Standard-price validation** — customer accepts MXN $12,500/month or equivalent annual economics because demonstrated ROI supports it.
6. **Repricing authority** — only after multiple external customers, renewals, operator-effort/gross-margin data and measured customer value.

## Founding-cohort financial illustration

- 3 founding pilots × MXN $9,900 = **MXN $29,700 one-time revenue**
- 2 Pilot → Guard conversions × MXN $6,900 = **MXN $13,800 MRR** during the founding period
- if both later validate standard Guard pricing: **MXN $25,000 MRR / MXN $300,000 ARR run-rate**

The strategically valuable event is not merely selling the first pilot. It is proving that unrelated external organizations pay recurrently for Change Intelligence and renew because measured value exceeds price.

## Future pricing after evidence, NOT launch pricing
After ≥5 external customers, renewal and measured ROI:

- Team/Guard: ~MXN $15k–$25k/month
- Enterprise Change Intelligence: ~MXN $300k–$600k+/year

Do not publish these as current offers without evidence.

## Discount discipline

Never create a permanently cheap SKU merely to reduce sales anxiety.

Founding discounts must be:

- time-bounded or scope-bounded
- explicitly labeled as founding/design-partner terms
- tied to measurable commercial learning
- tracked separately from standard/list pricing

Discounts outside the founding structure require something economically valuable in return: annual prepay, case-study/reference rights, structured measurement, smaller scope or design-partner obligations.

# 11. Economic Separation: Change Intelligence / Operations OS ✅

Never value the same capability twice.

Use three buckets:

## OPS_OS
Merchant/POS/operations workflows, Tablet/PC/Mobile product surfaces, store/device/sale/cashier models, merchant pricing/subscriptions/licensing, customer contracts/renewals, merchant-facing runtime and PRISMA-specific adapters.

## CHANGE_INTELLIGENCE
Generalizable Code Atlas core, repository reality, architecture/dependency intelligence, coverage/important-file gates, generalized DB reality, change authority, Safe Scope/Do Not Touch/CAN_PATCH/Impact Radius, evidence confidence, continuation, generalized UIMAP/Surface Target concepts and Discover/Guard/Control commercial IP.

## SHARED_PLATFORM
Authority Mesh/AutoMesh, shared evidence orchestration, Factory Ledger/field-learning when company-internal, shared packaging/continuity, generic QA/release governance and CI/security/deployment substrate.

Shared assets count once at group level.

Economic ownership test:

1. If Operations OS vanished, could the capability still sell to unrelated software companies? → leans Change Intelligence.
2. If Change Intelligence vanished, would it still create direct merchant value? → leans Operations OS.
3. If both need it and neither owns the economic reason → Shared.

Track revenue and costs separately from the first peso:

- merchant revenue → OPS_OS
- Pilot/Guard/Control revenue → CHANGE_INTELLIGENCE

No legal spin-out needed yet unless investors, buyers, recurring revenue, liability/security or contracting requirements justify it.
