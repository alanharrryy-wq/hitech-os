# Recipes

The goal is to keep only a few final recipes. Old Liquid/Cloudglass docs are useful, but not all of them should be authority at once.

## Final recipe candidates

| Name | Status | Surfaces | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| tablet-cloudglass-light | finalize | tablet | light, tactile, luminous, background visible under governed panels |
| pc-graphite-glass | finalize | pc | dense backoffice graphite, analytics-first |
| mobile-mist | finalize | mobile | compact companion, pulse/alerts |
| glass-pill | distill | shared | capsule/pill interaction surface |
| liquid-panel | distill | pc/shared | panel recipe with optical discipline |
| operational-card | finalize | tablet/shared | cards that preserve readability and background air |

## Final recipe policy

- Tablet uses `tablet-cloudglass-light`: light, tactile, readable, background visible.
- PC uses `pc-graphite-glass`: dense, administrative, analytical.
- Mobile uses `mobile-mist`: compact, thumb-friendly, alert/pulse oriented.
- Glass pill/capsule becomes a component-level recipe, not a global blur rule.
- Liquid panel becomes a tokenized panel recipe, not a permission to transparentar todo a lo bruto.
